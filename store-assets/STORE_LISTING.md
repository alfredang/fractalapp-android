## Full description
Fractal turns a simple word or number into stunning, infinitely detailed fractal art — right on your Android phone and tablet.

Type any seed, like "nebula" or "1024", and Fractal instantly generates a unique, colorful image. The same seed always recreates the same artwork, so your favourites are easy to find again. Tap Surprise for an endless stream of new creations.

EXPLORE SIX FRACTAL FAMILIES
• Mandelbrot — the classic set with endless detail at every zoom
• Julia — flowing filaments and spirals shaped by a seed point
• Burning Ship — jagged, ship-like structures
• Tricorn — the mirrored "Mandelbar"
• Phoenix — swirling tendrils with a feedback term
• Celtic — heart-shaped lobes from a folded Mandelbrot

MAKE IT YOURS
• Eight vivid colour palettes: Aurora, Inferno, Ocean, Neon, Sunset, Viridis, Candy and Mono
• Fine-tune detail, hue, colour density and interior glow
• Drag to pan and pinch or double-tap to zoom deep into the structure
• Hand-tuned gallery of beautiful starting points

SAVE & SHARE
• Export high-resolution artwork straight to your gallery
• Share to your favourite apps, or set it as a wallpaper

FAST & PRIVATE
• Rendered live on your device's GPU with OpenGL ES
• Completely offline — no account, no network, no tracking
• Storage access is requested only when you choose to save an image

Whether you want a one-of-a-kind wallpaper, a relaxing way to explore mathematical beauty, or a creative tool for generative art, Fractal makes it effortless.
